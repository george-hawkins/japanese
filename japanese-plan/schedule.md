# Schedule

A unit is five A sessions and one B session, done in that order. The layouts of the sessions are in [sessions.md](sessions.md). This file says what is new in each unit. Tick a box for each completed session: the first five boxes are the A sessions and the sixth is the B session.

## Setup evening (before Unit 1)

1. Install the Kaishi 1.5k deck in Anki from the releases page of <https://github.com/donkuri/Kaishi> and set the deck options listed in [sessions.md](sessions.md).
2. Create a Bunpro account, which starts the 30-day trial, and choose the settings listed in [resources.md](resources.md).
3. Bookmark the Cure Dolly "Japanese from Scratch" playlist, the transcript, the Tadoku free books page and Natural Japanese.
4. In Natural Japanese, set the Watch page to Complete Beginner, "Unwatched Only" and sorted by "Easiest", which is the view the listening block starts from.
5. Order *A Dictionary of Basic Japanese Grammar* and buy the notebook.
6. Download the 2012 N5 practice workbook files (four question PDFs, audio, answer key) from <https://www.jlpt.jp/e/samples/sampleindex.html> into a folder, and do not open them.
7. Set up the Japanese input method on your Mac.

Notes on Phase 1:

- Cure Dolly's lessons run ahead of Bunpro. Lessons 13, 15 and 19 to 33 cover the passive, transitivity, the causative, conditionals and other structures that Bunpro teaches at N4. Treat these as a first exposure. You are not expected to retain them fully, and you will watch the important ones again in Phase 2.
- Cure Dolly teaches plain forms first and reaches です and ます in lesson 17, whereas Bunpro and the Tadoku books use です and ます from the start. Since you have met both before, this difference should cause no difficulty.
- In Unit 10, use part of the B session's listening block to read the free first episodes of "Human Japanese Extra Credit" on Satori Reader. Subscribe if it works for you.

Notes on Phase 2:

- *Making Sense of Japanese* is about 140 pages, so ten B sessions need about 14 pages each. Its examples are printed in romaji, which is a nuisance, but the book's arguments are what you are reading it for.
- N4 Lesson 5 has 23 points, most of which are small items of vocabulary such as ～代, 各 and 以上. It fits in two units at two to three points per session.
- N4 Lesson 7 introduces honorific and humble speech. This is the lesson most likely to need extra A sessions.
- Order the two Shin Kanzen Master N4 books in Unit 25. Set up Yomitan in the B session of Unit 25.
- If the first Satori Reader series is too hard in Unit 11, meaning that an episode regularly takes more than two sessions, read Tadoku Level 2 and Level 3 books in the reading block for two more units and then try again.

### Test 2 (T session after Unit 30) ☐

| Result | What to do in Phase 3 |
|---|---|

| Vocabulary or grammar-with-reading below 50% | Stop and review the plan with me before starting Phase 3, because a result that low means the earlier material has not been retained. |
| Everything 60% or more | Follow Phase 3 as written. |

## Phase 3: Bridge (Units 31–36)

No new Bunpro points are added in this phase. Bunpro reviews continue. Do not start Bunpro's N3 lessons, because that decision belongs to the N3 plan.

*Shin Kanzen Master N4 Grammar* consists of an introduction to the question types, nine form-practice sections, Part 1 with 25 lessons and 5 review tests, Part 2 with 15 lessons and 3 review tests, and a mock test. Counting each section, lesson and review test as one item, there are 57 items, and two items per A session cover them in 29 A sessions. The mock test takes the thirtieth.

## Ready for Shin Kanzen Master N3

The N3 grammar book is 159 pages. Its explanations are in Japanese with English and Chinese translations, and its exercises are entirely in Japanese. You are ready for it when all of the following are true:

- Test 3 meets the marks in the first row of its table.
- All 317 Bunpro points for N5 and N4 have been learned, and Bunpro reviews take 15 minutes or less.
- Kaishi 1.5k is finished and you have added several hundred words from your reading.
- You can read an episode from Satori Reader's Easier group with furigana hidden, tapping for only a minority of the words.
- You follow Beginner videos on Natural Japanese comfortably and Intermediate ones in outline.

At that point we review how the plan went and write the N3 plan, which is also where a tutor, native material such as anime, and Migaku come back into consideration.
